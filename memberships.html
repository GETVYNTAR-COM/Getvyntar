Questions

Before you ask…

I've never touched a kettlebell. Can I still join?

Absolutely — most of our members started exactly there. Every new member goes through our Foundations onboarding, where a coach teaches you the core movements at your pace before you join the main timetable.

How do I book classes?

Booking currently runs through our class booking system — you'll get access when you join. A brand-new members' area with booking and payments built into this website is on the way.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes. Holidays, injuries, life — it happens. Flex and Unlimited plans can be paused for up to two months a year at no charge. Just drop us a message.

What should I bring to a class?

Flat-soled trainers (or bare feet — very kettlebell), comfortable clothes and a water bottle. We provide everything else, including the chalk and the encouragement.

Is there an age limit?

Members range from late teens to well past retirement. Kettlebell training scales to any level — the bell just gets lighter or heavier. Under-18s train with parental consent.
