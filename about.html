About U38

Built in a unit.
Powered by people.

U38 started with a simple idea: strip training back to what works — kettlebells, coaching and consistency — and build a place people actually want to train.

Our Story

It started with one bell and a big idea.

Unit 38 wasn't much to look at when we got the keys — a bare industrial unit with a concrete floor and a lot of potential. That was the point.

We didn't want mirrors, machines or a sales desk. We wanted a training floor where the coaching is the product. A few bells became a full rack. A few friends became 65+ members. The floor got chalkier, the community got stronger, and U38 became what it is today: a boutique strength studio that punches well above its square footage.

What We Believe

Simple. Hard. Effective.

Strength is a skill

Swings, presses, squats, carries. We teach them properly, load them progressively, and repeat them until they're yours.

Standards over shortcuts

We'd rather you press a lighter bell beautifully than a heavy one badly. Quality earns load at U38 — every time.

Training for life

Most of our members aren't athletes — they're parents, tradespeople, desk workers. We train for the life outside the studio.

Nobody trains alone

Every class, every level, every age — you're part of the same crew. New faces get welcomed, not sized up.

What To Expect

Your first session, demystified.

  • A proper welcome — your coach greets you, learns your goals and any niggles before you touch a bell.
  • Technique-first warm-up — we groove the movements of the day before adding any load.
  • The work — a programmed strength block and a conditioning finisher, scaled precisely to you.
  • No lost sheep — with 12 people max, your coach is never more than a few steps away.
