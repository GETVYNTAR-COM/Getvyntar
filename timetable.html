The Classes

What each class means.

Kettlebell Strength

Our flagship class. Presses, squats, deadlifts and swings in a programmed strength cycle. 45 minutes, all levels.

Kettlebell Conditioning

Swings, snatches, carries and circuits that build an engine. Hard, scalable and strangely addictive.

Foundations

The beginner's on-ramp. Learn the core kettlebell lifts from scratch in a small, patient, zero-pressure group.

Mobility & Restore

The class that keeps you training. Joint work, breathing and movement quality — your future self says thanks.

Team Session

Saturday's partner-and-team workout. The loudest hour of the week and the fastest 60 minutes in fitness.

Lunch Express & Open Gym

A 30-minute midday hit for busy schedules, plus Saturday open gym for members who want extra practice time.
