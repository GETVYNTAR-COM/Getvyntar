Still Unsure?

The questions everyone asks.

Do I need to be fit before I start?

No — that's what we're for. Every exercise scales from "first day ever" to "seasoned lifter", and your coach picks the right version for you. Starting is the only requirement.

Will I be the only new person?

Very unlikely — new members join every month, and the free trial classes we recommend always have a mix of levels. Nobody will be watching you; they're too busy with their own bells.

Is there really no catch?

Really. No card details, no contract, no pushy follow-up. We're confident the session sells itself — and if it's not for you, that's genuinely fine.
