The Coaches

Coaching is the product.

Equipment is easy to buy. Great coaching isn't. Every U38 class is led by a coach who knows your name, your history and exactly which cue you need to hear.

The Standard

What "coach-led" actually means here.

  • Qualified and current — every coach holds recognised kettlebell and personal training certifications, and keeps them sharp.
  • Eyes on every rep — 12 people max per class means nobody trains unseen.
  • Coaching, not counting — we cue, correct and scale in real time, not just shout the clock.
  • Your history matters — injuries, goals and preferences travel with you from class to class.
