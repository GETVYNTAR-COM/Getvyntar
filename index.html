01 — Why U38

Not a gym. A strength studio.

No rows of treadmills. No sales floor. Just kettlebells, a coach who knows your name, and a program that actually goes somewhere.

Coach-led, always

Every session is run by a qualified coach. Technique first, load second — you'll never be left guessing at a whiteboard.

Small group training

Capped at 12 people per class. Enough energy to push you, small enough that your coach sees every rep.

Programmed progression

Structured strength cycles, not random workouts. You'll know what you're building towards — and you'll feel it happen.

A community that shows up

65+ members who know each other's names, celebrate PBs, and notice when you miss a week. That's the difference.
